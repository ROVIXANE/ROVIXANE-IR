First-time setup

  1. Connect your phone to the ROVIXANE-IR-Setup Wi-Fi network.
  2. Open http://192.168.4.1 in your browser.
  3. Open Settings and enter your Wi-Fi network name and password.
  4. Save, restart, then reconnect your phone to your own Wi-Fi network.

If your phone says there is no internet, keep using this network so setup can continue.

No buttons yet. Add a button or learn one from your remote.

Learn remote signal

Point your remote at the TSOP38238 receiver and press a button. The dashboard shows the decoded protocol, HEX value, bit length, carrier frequency and Raw timing capture.
